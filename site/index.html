The 3D printer
in your pocket.

Find a model on Printables, Thingiverse or MakerWorld, tap print – PocketPrint3D slices it with OrcaSlicer and sends it to your own printer. For open printers, without a manufacturer cloud. Free and open source.

Get started Open in your browser Source code

Android app in beta testing · in your browser at app.pocketprint3d.com · iOS app in development · free cloud or your own server (Unraid, Home Assistant, Docker)

What it does

On your computer too

The same app and account in your browser at app.pocketprint3d.com: search, prepare, 3D preview, spools – just drag a model file onto the page. Printing from the browser goes through a bridge at home.

Send from OrcaSlicer

Slicing on your computer? Add PocketPrint3D to OrcaSlicer as a physical printer (Octo/Klipper). Your desktop prints show up as jobs, reach the printer through your bridge and book the right spool – every print in one place.

Set up in seconds

When you add a printer, the app searches your Wi-Fi: Centauri Carbon, Klipper/COSMOS, Prusa (PrusaLink) and OctoPrint show up in a list. One tap and it's set up – no IP addresses to look up. (Android; iOS follows.)

Find and print

Search Printables and Thingiverse in the app, or open printables.com with your own login to see your likes and collections. MakerWorld models too: open the link in the app, download the 3MF in MakerWorld and share it back.

Your own library

Running Manyfold at home? Your model collection shows up as a search source next to Printables – search, pictures, print.

Sliced for you

OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 with the right profile for your printer – about 1000 printer models and a large filament library built in. Print time, filament, a 3D view of the model and a 2D/3D preview of the sliced layers before anything starts.

Pictures on the printer

The G-code carries a preview image in the sizes and formats your printer's screen expects (PNG, QOI for Prusa) – you see what's printing right on the printer.

Exact times for Klipper

For Klipper printers the print time is recalculated with your printer's own speed and acceleration limits (klipper_estimator) – often 10–20 % closer than the slicer.

Your printer, live

Progress, temperatures, pause, resume and cancel. With your own server or a bridge also camera, temperature charts, fans, light, speed and switching the printer on or off through Home Assistant.

Print from anywhere

Your PocketPrint3D server at home – Unraid, Home Assistant, Docker or a small Raspberry Pi – becomes a bridge: enter its code in the app once, and you reach its printers from anywhere. Status, camera pictures, controls and printing over mobile data. Outgoing connection only: no port forwarding, no VPN, your printers stay off the internet.

Time-lapse

Switch it on before printing: one camera picture per layer, a short video when the print is done – watch and download it in the app or browser. With your own server or a bridge and the printer's camera.

AI failure detection

With Obico's ML API on your server, camera pictures are checked while printing; a likely failure (spaghetti, detaching) shows in the app and can pause the print – also on the Centauri Carbon with stock firmware.

Multicolour

Choose the slot of your AFC unit (e.g. CANVAS) for every colour – the material follows the filament in the slot.

Spoolman & NFC spools

Pick the spool per colour, get a warning when one won't be enough, the used filament is booked after the print. Add spools from the open SpoolmanDB or by holding the phone to an OpenPrintTag NFC spool. No Spoolman at home? Keep your spools in your account.

Your own profiles

Upload your OrcaSlicer printer, quality and material profiles – or paste the link of a public Orca Cloud bundle. They are used for slicing right away.

Safe by design

Nothing starts without your confirmation. Your printer is never exposed to the internet; with the cloud, its address and passwords stay on your phone – or, with a bridge, are encrypted so that only your server at home can read them.

Free

No subscription, no ads, no tracking. Open source under the MIT licence.

Two ways to use it

PocketPrint3D Cloud – nothing to install at home

  1. Install the app and log in with your e-mail address (you get a 6-digit code, no password).
  2. Add your printer: the app finds it on your home Wi-Fi – tap it, done (or enter its address yourself). The address stays on your phone.
  3. Print: slicing runs on our server in Germany; at home the app sends the G-code over your Wi-Fi straight to the printer.
  4. On your computer (optional): open app.pocketprint3d.com and log in with the same e-mail. Slicing in OrcaSlicer? Printer → Send from OrcaSlicer in the app gives you the address and key for OrcaSlicer's physical printer.
  5. Away from home (optional): run a bridge at home – the small printshare-bridge Docker image, or your own PocketPrint3D server with Connect to PocketPrint3D Cloud switched on – and enter its code in the app under Settings → Advanced → Print from anywhere.

Your own server – everything stays at home

  1. Install the PocketPrint3D server on Unraid (template), Home Assistant (add-on) or any Docker host.
  2. Connect the app: open the server's web page, go to Settings → Connect the app and scan the QR code.
  3. Print – at home, and from anywhere: switch on Connect to PocketPrint3D Cloud and your server becomes a bridge for your cloud account (or use Tailscale). Only with your own server: your Manyfold library, exact Klipper times and AI failure detection.

Both are free. Step by step: Help (getting started and advanced). Installation of your own server: GitHub.
