Getting started

You need: your 3D printer switched on and connected to your Wi-Fi (Elegoo Centauri Carbon, a Klipper printer, a Prusa with PrusaLink or an OctoPrint setup), your phone on the same Wi-Fi, and an e-mail address. Nothing to install at home.
  1. Install the app

    Android: currently as a beta test in Google Play. iPhone: coming soon. On a computer you can use app.pocketprint3d.com with the same account.

  2. Sign up – just your e-mail

    Tap Sign up for free, enter your e-mail and type in the 6-digit code you receive. No password.

  3. Add your printer

    Tap Add printer. The app searches your Wi-Fi and lists your printer – tap it, then Save. Prusa: also enter the PrusaLink password (printer screen → Settings → Network). OctoPrint: an API key (OctoPrint → Settings → Application Keys).

  4. Find a model

    In Discover search Printables or Thingiverse. Or share a model link from your browser with PocketPrint3D, or open a file (STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP) with Choose a file.

  5. Prepare the print

    Choose material and quality – the defaults fit most models. Supports only for overhangs. Then Prepare print: after about a minute you see print time, filament and a preview.

  6. Print

    Check the preview, tap Print and confirm that the build plate is empty and the right material is loaded. Nothing ever starts without this confirmation.

  7. Watch it

    The Printers tab shows progress, remaining time and temperatures. Pause, resume or cancel there. In Jobs the print shows as “Printing”, then “Printed” or “Cancelled”. Want it again? Open the job and tap Print again.

If something doesn't work

The printer isn't found
Is it switched on and is your phone on the same Wi-Fi (not on mobile data, not on a guest network)? Tap Search again, or Enter the address yourself – the printer shows its IP address in its network menu.
"Not reachable"
At home the app talks to the printer directly. Away from home this only works with a bridge (see below).
The file is on the printer but the print didn't start
Some printers ignore the start command now and then. Start the file on the printer's screen – it is already there.
The job still says “Printing” although the print is done
The job learns it from the printer: through a bridge within a minute, without a bridge when your phone is home and the app shows the Printers tab. Open it once on your home Wi-Fi.
The model is too big or slicing fails
Try a smaller size (Size in "On the plate") or another file of the model.

Print from anywhere (bridge)

New to this? Set up a bridge on a Raspberry Pi – shopping list (about €60) and a ready-made card image: write it, plug in cable and power, tap “Connect” in the app.
  1. Run a bridge at home: on your own PocketPrint3D server switch on Connect to PocketPrint3D Cloud (Unraid template, Home Assistant add-on option, or the server's web page → Settings). Without a server: the small Docker image ghcr.io/halvar20000/printshare-bridge.
  2. The bridge shows a code (container log or its web page).
  3. In the app: Settings → Advanced → Print from anywhere, enter the code, Connect.
  4. The bridge's printers appear in your account, marked "through a bridge". More printers: Add a printer through the bridge – address and password are encrypted so only your bridge can read them.

Only an outgoing connection: nothing to open in your router, your printers stay off the internet.

On your computer

  • Open app.pocketprint3d.com and log in with the same e-mail – same printers, jobs and spools.
  • Drag a model file onto the page to start.
  • Printing from the browser works for printers behind a bridge. Others are marked "Phone only": start them in the app, or download the G-code.

Send from OrcaSlicer

Why through PocketPrint3D? OrcaSlicer can often reach your printer directly – but then the print bypasses PocketPrint3D. Through PocketPrint3D:
  • Spools stay right: the used filament is booked like for every other print.
  • From anywhere: slice on the laptop at work, the printer at home prints – through your bridge.
  • One overview: the print shows up in the app and the browser – preview, progress, camera, pause.
  • Check first, start later: “Upload” gets the print ready; you start it on your phone once the plate is clear, and choose spool and slot there.

You don't manage spools and only print at home? Then sending directly from OrcaSlicer is just as good. You don't add a new printer in OrcaSlicer – you give your real printer another way: through PocketPrint3D.

  1. In the app or browser: Settings → your printer → Send from OrcaSlicer on your computer → Create access. You get an address and a key (shown once).
  2. In OrcaSlicer: Wi-Fi icon next to the printer (add physical printer) → host type Octo/Klipper → enter address and key → Test.
  3. After slicing: Upload – the print waits as a job in the app. Upload and print – starts right away on a printer behind a bridge and books the spool used last.

Filament spools

  • Settings → Advanced → Filament spools: keep your spools in your PocketPrint3D account, or connect your own Spoolman.
  • When printing, choose the spool per colour. You get a warning if one isn't enough or the material doesn't match.
  • The used filament is booked when the print is finished. With spools in the account this also works when the app is closed (printers behind a bridge).
  • New spools: pick them from the filament database, or hold the phone to an OpenPrintTag NFC spool (Android).

Time-lapse

  • On the review screen switch on Record a time-lapse (off by default). The switch appears for printers with a camera that your own server or a bridge reaches.
  • While printing, one picture is taken per layer (Centauri Carbon, Klipper) or every 30 seconds (Prusa, OctoPrint).
  • When the print is done, a short video is made. On the job: Watch the time-lapse – with Download the video. It is deleted with the job after 14 days.

Multicolour (AFC / CANVAS)

For multicolour models choose the slot for every colour before preparing; empty slots are flagged, and the material follows the filament in the slot. Supported on Klipper printers with AFC (e.g. Centauri Carbon with COSMOS + CANVAS).

Your own OrcaSlicer profiles

Settings → your printer → Printer profile: upload your OrcaSlicer printer, quality and material profiles, or paste the link of a public Orca Cloud bundle. They are used for slicing right away.

Extras with your own server

Your model library
Manyfold at home shows up as a search source next to Printables.
AI failure detection
With Obico's ML API, camera pictures are checked while printing; a likely failure can pause the print.
Printer on/off
A smart plug in Home Assistant switches the printer on and off from the app.
Exact times for Klipper
Print times recalculated with your printer's own limits (klipper_estimator).
